## Full answers

### 1. [PASS] (factual) What is the benefit under PM-KISAN?

Under the **PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi)** scheme, eligible farmer families receive Rs. 6,000 per year. This amount is paid in three equal installments of Rs. 2,000 every four months and is transferred directly to the farmers' bank accounts via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).

You can find more information or verify details at the official link: https://pmkisan.gov.in

### 2. [PASS] (factual) How much premium do I pay for PM Suraksha Bima Yojana?

For the Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY), you pay an annual premium of Rs. 20. This amount is auto-debited from your linked bank account.

For more information, you can visit the official website: https://jansuraksha.gov.in

### 3. [PASS] (factual) What documents are needed for e-Shram registration?

To register for the **e-Shram (National Database of Unorganised Workers)** scheme, you will need the following documents:

*   **Aadhaar card** (linked to your mobile number)
*   **Bank account details**
*   **Self-declared occupation details**
*   **Educational qualification details** (if applicable)

If your Aadhaar is not linked to a mobile number, you can complete the registration at a Common Service Centre (CSC) using biometric authentication. 

For more information and to apply, please visit the official website: https://eshram.gov.in

### 4. [PASS] (factual) What is the maximum loan amount under PM Mudra Yojana?

The maximum loan amount under the Pradhan Mantri Mudra Yojana (PMMY) is Rs. 20 lakh, which is available under the "Tarun Plus" category for repeat borrowers who have a clean repayment record on an earlier Tarun loan.

For more information, you can visit the official website: https://www.mudra.org.in

### 5. [PASS] (factual) Who is eligible for PM Vishwakarma Yojana?

To be eligible for the **PM Vishwakarma Yojana**, you must meet the following criteria:

*   **Profession:** You must be an artisan or craftsperson working with your hands and tools in one of the 18 recognized trade categories (such as carpenters, blacksmiths, potters, goldsmiths, cobblers, tailors, and others).
*   **Age:** You must be at least 18 years old.
*   **Experience:** There is no minimum number of years of practice required; a self-declaration with photo evidence is sufficient.
*   **Household Limit:** Only one member per family can enroll in the scheme.
*   **Prior Subsidies:** Your family must not have availed of similar credit-based subsidies (such as PMEGP, PM SVANidhi, or MUDRA) in the last 5 years.
*   **Employment Status:** Government employees and their family members are not eligible.

For further information and to verify your eligibility, please visit the official website: https://pmvishwakarma.gov.in

### 7. [PASS] (factual) How do I apply for Ayushman Bharat PMJAY?

### 9. [PASS] (comparison) What is the difference between PM Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana and PM Suraksha Bima Yojana?

The main differences between the **Pradhan Mantri Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana (PMJJBY)** and the **Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY)** are as follows:

*   **Nature of Insurance:**
    *   **PMJJBY:** A life insurance scheme that provides coverage for death due to **any cause** (natural, illness, or accident).
    *   **PMSBY:** An accidental insurance scheme that provides coverage specifically for **accidental death or disability**.
*   **Age Eligibility:**
    *   **PMJJBY:** Available to citizens aged **18 to 50 years** (coverage continues until age 55).
    *   **PMSBY:** Available to citizens aged **18 to 70 years**.
*   **Premium Cost:**
    *   **PMJJBY:** The annual premium is **Rs. 436**.
    *   **PMSBY:** The annual premium is **Rs. 20**.
*   **Benefits:**
    *   **PMJJBY:** Offers a life insurance cover of **Rs. 2 lakh**.
    *   **PMSBY:** Offers Rs. 2 lakh for accidental death or total/irrecoverable disability, and Rs. 1 lakh for partial disability.

Both schemes are government-backed, renewable annually, and require a savings bank or post office account with auto-debit consent. You can find more authoritative and up-to-date information at their official website: [https://jansuraksha.gov.in](https://jansuraksha.gov.in).

### 12. [PASS] (comparison) Should I apply for Atal Pension Yojana or the NSAP old age pension scheme?

Whether you should apply for the **Atal Pension Yojana (APY)** or the **Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme (IGNOAPS)** depends on your current age, financial status, and the nature of the support you are looking for. Here are the key differences based on the provided information:

### **Atal Pension Yojana (APY)**
*   **Purpose:** A voluntary, contributory scheme for workers in the unorganised sector to build a guaranteed monthly pension for themselves after age 60.
*   **Eligibility:** You must be an Indian citizen between **18 and 40 years old** and hold an active savings bank or post office account. You must **not** be an income tax payer.
*   **How it works:** You make regular contributions (monthly, quarterly, or half-yearly) during your working years, which are auto-debited.
*   **Official Link:** [https://www.jansuraksha.gov.in](https://www.jansuraksha.gov.in)

### **Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme (IGNOAPS)**
*   **Purpose:** A social welfare scheme providing financial assistance to elderly persons from households living Below Poverty Line (BPL).
*   **Eligibility:** You must be **60 years of age or above** and belong to a **Below Poverty Line (BPL) household** (verified via SECC-2011 data or state BPL lists). You are not eligible if you are already receiving a similar pension from another Central or State Government scheme.
*   **How it works:** It is a government-funded pension; it does not require you to make contributions.
*   **Official Link:** [https://nsap.nic.in](https://nsap.nic.in)

### **Key Comparison for Your Decision:**
*   **If you are under 40:** You are eligible for the **Atal Pension Yojana**, which allows you to save for your own future pension.
*   **If you are 60 or older and meet the BPL criteria:** You may be eligible for **IGNOAPS**, which provides direct financial assistance to senior citizens in need.

Please check the official websites provided above for more detailed information and to verify the specific eligibility criteria that apply to your current situation.

### 14. [PASS] (comparison) What's the difference between Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana and Vahli Dikri Yojana for a Gujarat family?

For a family in Gujarat, the **Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY)** and the **Vahli Dikri Yojana** differ in their objectives, funding structures, and application criteria. Here are the key differences based on the provided details:

### **1. Purpose and Nature**
*   **Vahli Dikri Yojana:** A Gujarat state government scheme aimed at reducing school dropouts, promoting girls' education, and improving the child sex ratio through staged financial assistance.
*   **Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY):** A central government small savings scheme designed to help parents build a financial corpus for a girl child's higher education and marriage through long-term, tax-free savings.

### **2. Financial Benefit Structure**
*   **Vahli Dikri Yojana:** The government provides a total grant of **Rs. 1,10,000 to Rs. 1,16,000** in specific stages:
    *   Class 1 admission (approx. Rs. 4,000).
    *   Class 9 admission (approx. Rs. 6,000–10,000).
    *   A lump sum of Rs. 1 lakh when the girl turns 18.
*   **Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana:** This is a **savings-based** scheme. The parent deposits money (between Rs. 250 and Rs. 1.5 lakh per financial year for 15 years), which earns an attractive interest rate (currently 8.2% p.a. for Q2 FY 2026-27). The maturity amount is the total of your deposits plus the accumulated interest.

### **3. Eligibility Criteria**
*   **Vahli Dikri Yojana:**
    *   **Family Income:** Annual family income must not exceed Rs. 2 lakh.
    *   **Birth Date:** The girl child must be born on or after 02-08-2019.
    *   **Limits:** Applicable only for the first two girl children.
*   **Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana:**
    *   **Age:** The account must be opened for a girl child below 10 years of age.
    *   **Income/Limits:** There is no family income limit. A maximum of two accounts are allowed per family (one per girl, with exceptions for twins/triplets).

### **4. Application Process**
*   **Vahli Dikri Yojana:** Applications are submitted at the local **Anganwadi Kendra** or the **District Office of the Women and Child Development Department**.
*   **Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana:** Applications are processed through any **authorised bank branch** or **post office**.

For more information and to verify eligibility for your specific situation, please refer to the official websites:
*   **Vahli Dikri Yojana:** [https://wcd.gujarat.gov.in](https://wcd.gujarat.gov.in)
*   **Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana:** [https://www.nsiindia.gov.in/InternalPage.aspx?Id_Pk=89](https://www.nsiindia.gov.in/InternalPage.aspx?Id_Pk=89)
